## Design encoded

Pre-60S particles were purified via Arx1-TAP from a thermosensitive *drg1-18* *S. cerevisiae* mutant, incubated with purified Drg1 + ATPγS, then split: DSS-d0/d12, BS3-d0/d12, or no crosslinking. Only the two crosslinked thirds were deposited as RAW.

`factor value[cross-linker]` is `DSS` vs `BS3`.
