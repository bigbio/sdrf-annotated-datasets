## Design encoded

- Three treatments after 24 h: mock (medium only), TcnA (255 ng/mL), formaldehyde-inactivated TcnA (`FA_TcnA`). Three biological replicates each; TMT131N is a pool of the three TcnA lysates.
- After TMT pooling: 1 mg equivalent → proteome high-pH fractions (`*_P_*`, 9 RAW including flow-through/wash); 5 mg equivalent → TiO2 + Fe-NTA phosphopeptide enrichment then high-pH fractions (`*_PP_*`, 9 RAW).
- Submitter fraction IDs were 1–9 in **each** series (ftw=1, `_01`=2, … `_08`=9). Proteome keeps 1–9. Phosphoproteome is numbered 10–18 so `(source name, biological replicate, technical replicate, fraction identifier)` is unique across both workflows. Assay name = RAW stem.
- `factor value[treatment]` and `factor value[enrichment process]` copy the corresponding characteristics.

## Technical metadata

- Instrument: Orbitrap Fusion Lumos (`MS:1002732`)
- Acquisition: `NT=Data-dependent acquisition;AC=PRIDE:0000627`
- Digestion: Lys-C then trypsin (paper); MaxQuant proteome search `Trypsin/P` + `LysC`. Dual cleavage columns: `Trypsin/P` (`MS:1001313`), `Lys-C` (`MS:1001309`)
- Reduction DTT (`PRIDE:0000608`); alkylation IAA (`PRIDE:0000599`) → Carbamidomethyl C fixed (`UNIMOD:4`)
- TMT6plex N-term and K fixed (`UNIMOD:737`; TMT10plex uses the same reagent mass)
- Variable (both mqpar files): Oxidation M (`UNIMOD:35`), Acetyl protein N-term (`UNIMOD:1`), Deamidated NQ (`UNIMOD:7`), HexNAc T (`UNIMOD:43`; paper N-acetylglucosaminylation on T)
- Phospho STY (`UNIMOD:21`) only on phosphoproteome rows (`not applicable` on proteome rows, matching `mqpar_phosphoproteom.xml` vs `mqpar_proteom.xml`)
- Dissociation: HCD as `NT=beam-type collision-induced dissociation;AC=MS:1000422`; collision energy `38 NCE`
- Precursor `4.5 ppm` (MaxQuant `mainSearchTol`); fragment `20 ppm` (FTMS `MatchTolerance`)
- Fractionation: high-pH reversed-phase (`PRIDE:0000564`)
- Culture medium: MEM (paper: Gibco minimal essential medium). OLS NCIT has no matching MEM term; validator warns
- Sample storage: −80 °C (cells frozen overnight before lysis)
- Passage number and cell-line authentication: `not available`
