## Experiment (from PRIDE + paper Methods)

HyPR-MS (hybridization purification of RNA–protein complexes followed by MS) on:

- Patient-derived prostate tumor xenografts **LuCaP 77 (XG77)** and **LuCaP 147 (XG147)**: MALAT1 capture, scrambled oligonucleotide negative control, then poly(A) capture from the same lysates (3 biological replicates each).
- Mouse liver: poly(A) capture only (3 biological replicates; each from a different mouse).

Label-free trypsin digest (eFASP), Easy-nLC 1200 + **Orbitrap Fusion Lumos**, MetaMorpheus default settings, shotgun / DDA. Xenograft searches used the human Swiss-Prot database; liver searches used mouse Swiss-Prot.

- `human` v1.1.0 — 18/21 rows are human PDX tissue; human-required `disease`, `age`, and `sex` are present on all rows

Not used:

- `vertebrates` — mutually exclusive with `human`; mouse liver rows still use `Mus musculus` with `not available` / `not applicable` demographics
- `cell-lines` — LuCaP 77/147 are PDX **tissues**, not cultured cell lines; Cellosaurus search found no `LuCaP 77` or `LuCaP 147` records
- `dia-acquisition` — shotgun / DDA, not DIA
- `crosslinking` — formaldehyde is used to stabilize RNA–protein complexes, not XL-MS of crosslinked peptides
- `oncology-metadata` / `clinical-metadata` — no tumor stage, grade, or per-patient clinical table

## Gaps / review notes

- Donor **age**, **sex**, and **ancestry** for LuCaP 77/147 are not in the paper or Cellosaurus; set to `not available` (ancestry `not applicable` on mouse rows). Prostate PDX models are typically from male patients, but that was not stated in the sources used here.
- Mouse liver **strain, sex, and age** are not reported.
- **Cell type** is `not available` (bulk tissue).
- Xenograft biological replicates may pool 1–3 PDX pieces of the same line (paper); per-replicate pooling is not itemized, so `pooled` was not assigned.
- Dissociation method, mass tolerances, and alkylation/reduction reagent names are not stated; not invented.
- Formaldehyde RNA–protein crosslinking is experimental context only; XL-MS template not applied.
- Mixed-species file: `human` template is declared because most rows are human PDX; mouse rows keep `Mus musculus`.
