## Disease mapping

Spreadsheet → OLS (characteristics = bare labels). Two `characteristics[disease]` columns so primary hand eczema is not replaced by concurrent AD:

| Spreadsheet | n | Disease 1 (hand eczema status) | Disease 2 (concurrent AD) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hand eczema | 35 | `hand eczema` (EFO:0022959) | `normal` (PATO:0000461) |
| Hand eczema with atopic dermatitis | 33 | `hand eczema` (EFO:0022959) | `atopic eczema` (MONDO:0004980; EFO label, synonym atopic dermatitis) |
| Healthy control | 13 | `normal` | `normal` |

Dyshidrosis / pompholyx (MONDO:0006540) was **not** used. AD is not written as the first disease: that would drop the primary hand-eczema diagnosis.

Two `factor value[disease]` columns copy the two characteristic columns. `factor value[organism part]` copies anatomy (palm vs dorsal hand vs upper arm).
