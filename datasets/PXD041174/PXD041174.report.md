Not used:

## Ontology / technical values

Characteristics (bare labels, OLS-verified):

- organism: `Homo sapiens` (NCBITaxon:9606)
- organism part: `aorta` (UBERON:0000947)
- cell type: `aortic smooth muscle cell` (CL:0002539)
- disease: `normal` (PATO:0000461)
- material type: `cell`

Comments (NT+AC):

- instrument: `NT=Orbitrap Fusion;AC=MS:1002416` (OLS label is not “Tribrid”)
- label: `NT=label free sample;AC=MS:1002038`
- acquisition: `NT=Data-dependent acquisition;AC=PRIDE:0000627`
- cleavage: `NT=Trypsin;AC=MS:1001251`
- dissociation: `NT=beam-type collision-induced dissociation;AC=MS:1000422` (HCD)
- collision energy: `35 eV` (PRIDE processing protocol)
- reduction: `NT=Dithiothreitol (DTT);AC=PRIDE:0000608`
- alkylation: `NT=Iodoacetamide (IAA);AC=PRIDE:0000599`
- mods: Carbamidomethyl C fixed (UNIMOD:4); Oxidation M variable (UNIMOD:35); Acetyl protein N-term variable (UNIMOD:1); Met-loss protein N-term variable (UNIMOD:765)
- MS1 range: 375–1500 m/z from the PRIDE protocol

Compound / factor: spreadsheet free text copied into `characteristics[compound]` and `factor value[compound]`. ChEBI/NCIT have DMSO (`dimethyl sulfoxide`, CHEBI:28262), DMEM (NCIT:C185409), Electronic Cigarette (NCIT:C120482), and Heated Tobacco Product (NCIT:C175552), but not clean terms for “electronic cigarette extract”, “tobacco heating product extract”, or “cigarette smoke condensate”. Those IDs were not invented.
