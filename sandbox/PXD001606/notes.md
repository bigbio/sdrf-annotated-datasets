# PXD001606

BLOCKED: MALDI/ESI identification of 49 DIGE gel plugs; DAT vs non-DAT cannot be mapped to MS files.

## Why this is in sandbox
- Vendor `.wiff` files are gel-plug identifications from 2D-GE (S1 Table: 49 spots of interest), not per-animal plasma runs.
- Training-set DIGE compared chronic DAT vs non-DAT plasma (n=32); those gels are not the deposited TripleTOF files.
- No table maps Plug1–49 to an individual sea lion or disease class.

## Return criteria
- A cited map from gel plug / spot number to animal ID and DAT status, or deposition of the DIGE image quantitation as the primary assay.
