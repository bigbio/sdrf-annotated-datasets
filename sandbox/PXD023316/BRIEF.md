## Sample processing protocol
The platelet samples were completely lysed by ultrasound (120s, 4 s on and 6 s off) after adding the lysis buffer (8 M urea, pH 8.0, 1 cocktail, 1 mM PMSF), and then lysed on ice for 30 min, and centrifuged at 12000g for 10 minutes to obtain the pure protein solution
