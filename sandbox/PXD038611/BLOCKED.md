# PXD038611: duplicate of PXD039206

All 24 vendor RAW files of PXD038611 (`raw_control1_01.raw` … `raw_50_2_04.raw`; *G. speciosum* extract on RAW 264.7 cells) are byte-identical to those of PXD039206: same file names, sizes and PRIDE checksums. PXD039206 carries the linked publication (Data Brief 2023, PMC9932674) and is annotated as `sandbox/PXD039206/` in bangkok-2. PXD038611 has no publication, and differs only by an extra `raw_cells.mzData` and its own merged mzML/mzid.

No separate SDRF is emitted. It should be annotated by copying PXD039206's SDRF once that is reviewed.
