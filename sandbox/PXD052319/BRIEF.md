# PXD052319: sandbox brief

**Dataset:** MicroRNAs Biomarkers and Pathway Enrichment Analysis in Severe Pulmonary Hemorrhagic Syndrome due to Leptospirosis: A Multi-Omics and Multi-Center Study. Bangkok (bangkok-2).

**Status:** draft, not reviewed. It is in `sandbox/` because of the open point below.

## Evidence and decisions

Leptospirosis plasma pools, ZenoTOF 7600 DDA; NN_1-3, SN_1-3, SY_1-3 (MaxQuant experiments). Paper: non-SPHS analysed in six replicates, SPHS in three (S-Figure 6, supplement not retrievable: PMC blocks scripted download). Mapping NN+SN = non-SPHS, SY = SPHS is inferred from counts/codes -> sandbox. MaxQuant 0.6 Da main search, carbamidomethyl fixed, ox M + acetyl N-term var. Data files = .wiff (.wiff.scan / .wiff2 companions). J Infect 2025 PMC11966573.
